# ScamRoad — Engineering Weaknesses & Improvement Backlog

This backlog records the 15 weakest technical points identified during the Pass 2 adversarial audit of ScamRoad, ranked by operational impact.

---

| Rank | Issue ID | Area | Weakness Description | Root Cause | Proposed Mitigation | Target Metric | Status |
| :---: | :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :---: |
| **1** | `IB-01` | Non-Asphalt Pavements | Sett stone and cobblestone mortar joints trigger false alligator crack detections. | Model trained primarily on asphalt; stone seams visually resemble crack networks. | Train a stone-paving binary filter or add sett-stone negative mining. | Pavement patch specificity $\ge 75\%$ | DOCUMENTED |
| **2** | `IB-02` | Fine Crack Recall | Longitudinal and transverse cracks have low recall in held-out split. | High confidence floor (0.65) tuned to prevent false alarms on clean asphalt. | Multi-head multi-scale crack feature pyramid with high-resolution tiling (SAHI). | Crack F1 $\ge 50\%$ | IDENTIFIED |
| **3** | `IB-03` | Monocular Volumetric Boundary | No calibrated 3D depth (cm) or volume ($m^3$) measurement. | Monocular camera perspective lack physical depth cues or ground-truth scale markers. | Integrate stereo vision or LiDAR ground-truth point cloud fusion. | Calibrated depth error $< 10\%$ | BOUNDED |
| **4** | `IB-04` | Non-Road Diagram Rejection | Vector infographics (e.g. distress diagrams) occasionally trigger detections. | Lack of explicit road semantic segmentation scene classifier before object detection. | Add lightweight road surface binary classifier (road vs non-road scene). | Non-road FP = 0 | PLANNED |
| **5** | `IB-05` | Low-Light & Night Performance | Images under 15 luminance units rejected by usability check. | Low contrast and high sensor ISO noise conceal pothole crater cavities. | Train low-light enhancement autoencoder or IR camera stream ingestion. | Low-light recall $\ge 70\%$ | BOUNDED |
| **6** | `IB-06` | High-Speed Motion Blur | High patrol vehicle speed (>80 km/h) causes severe motion blur. | Standard rolling shutter smartphone cameras exhibit frame tearing at low shutter speeds. | Enforce minimum camera shutter speed (1/500s) or global shutter sensor hardware. | Blur rejection rate $< 5\%$ | QUEUED |
| **7** | `IB-07` | Mobile PWA Foreground Constraint | Web capture halts when smartphone screen locks or browser tab backgrounds. | Web browser security limits background camera and WebAssembly execution. | Build native background service worker or headless edge daemon for Linux SBCs. | Background patrol capture | PLANNED |
| **8** | `IB-08` | Illustrative Rate Card | Repair costs are indicative formulas, not official Schedule of Rates (SoR). | Civil repair costs vary by district, material availability, and tender rates. | Support dynamic municipal SoR CSV upload per district deployment. | Municipal rate adherence | MITIGATED |
| **9** | `IB-09` | Standing Water Occlusion | Water-filled potholes during monsoon rain conceal bottom cavitation. | Water reflection masks dark cavity depression gradients. | Integrate ripple and specular reflection detection to flag submerged hazards. | Submerged pothole recall $\ge 80\%$ | FUTURE |
| **10** | `IB-10` | Physical Edge Hardware Verification | On-device inference benchmarks currently measured on host AMD64 CPU. | Physical mobile devices (OPPO K13, Jetson Orin) require hands-on physical access. | Execute physical hardware test runs queued in `docs/HUMAN_TODO.md` (H-002). | Physical device FPS log | QUEUED |
| **11** | `IB-11` | High-Resolution Input Downsampling | 4K dashcam video is letterboxed to 640x640, discarding micro-texture. | Standard neural network input size trade-off for real-time edge throughput. | Implement Sliced Aided Hyper Inference (SAHI) on high-resolution frames. | Micro-crack detection $+15\%$ | FUTURE |
| **12** | `IB-12` | GPU Acceleration Parity | Pipeline currently executes on CPUExecutionProvider (ONNX FP32/INT8). | Designed for ubiquitous commodity hardware deployment without GPU dependencies. | Provide optional CUDA / TensorRT execution providers in Docker containers. | Latency $< 15$ ms on GPU | PLANNED |
| **13** | `IB-13` | Over-the-Air Model Updates | Edge agents require manual restart and model file replacement. | Static model directory structure without automated checkpoint synchronization. | Implement authenticated S3/MinIO model manifest polling and hot-reloading. | Zero-downtime model update | FUTURE |
| **14** | `IB-14` | Video Ego-Motion Compensation | In-video tracker uses 2D spatial IoU without camera motion compensation. | Vehicle acceleration, turns, and bumps cause sudden bounding box displacements. | Integrate optical flow homography matrix estimation into track predictor. | Track fragmentation $-20\%$ | FUTURE |
| **15** | `IB-15` | Multi-Camera Corroboration | System currently processes a single forward-facing dashcam stream. | Single monocular angle leaves road shoulders and blind spots uninspected. | Support dual wide-angle camera streams (forward + rear/side) with spatial fusion. | Corridor coverage $+40\%$ | FUTURE |
