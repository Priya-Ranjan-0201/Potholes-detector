● 01. PROBLEM STATEMENT 02 / 10

The Infrastructure Inspection Crisis

Why traditional municipal road surveys consistently fail commuters and taxpayers.

❌ Reactive Citizen Complaint Model

Surveys depend heavily on citizen complaints filed after damage occurs, leaving secondary and rural corridors un-inspected for months or years.

❌ Prohibitive $100K+ LiDAR Equipment

Specialized 3D laser profiler survey vans cost upwards of $100,000+ per unit, pricing out 98% of municipal town councils and public works departments.

❌ Subjective & Inconsistent Audits

Manual walking inspections produce qualitative, dispute-prone logs with zero cryptographic audit trails, delaying contractor repair work orders.

❌ Compounding Safety & Financial Costs

Neglected cavities trigger fatal commuter accidents and vehicle wear, multiplying asphalt reclamation costs by 5x compared to timely preventative sealing.

● 04. DEFECT TAXONOMY 05 / 10

Pavement Distress Classification & Taxonomy

Multi-class distress detection aligned with international civil engineering codes.

POTHOLE / CRATER CRITICAL

Bowl-shaped structural cavities with depth ≥ 25mm caused by moisture penetration under heavy wheelpath loads. Severe tire blowout and suspension hazard.

ALLIGATOR CRACKING SEVERE

Interconnected polygonal crack networks resembling reptile skin. Signals base course structural exhaustion; requires slurry seal or full milling.

LONGITUDINAL CRACK MODERATE

Linear fractures running parallel to pavement centerline, caused by paving joint fatigue and repetitive heavy axle wheel tracking.

TRANSVERSE CRACK MINOR

Fractures running perpendicular to road direction resulting from temperature contraction stresses and shrinkage of asphalt surface layer.
