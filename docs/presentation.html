01. Introduction & Context

Problem Statement: The Highway Safety & Road Inspection Crisis

THE REAL-WORLD PROBLEM

Public Safety & Financial Burden

Over 3,500+ Fatalities Annually

Potholes and sudden pavement depressions cause fatal vehicular accidents and two-wheeler crashes every year across Indian roads.

Massive Financial Drain (₹15,000+ Cr)

Public funds are repeatedly spent on poor quality road repairs that erode after a single monsoon season.

Severe Vehicle Wear & Traffic Bottlenecks

Damaged road surfaces damage suspensions, cause tire blowouts, and slow down commercial transport.

LIMITATIONS OF CURRENT METHODS

Why Manual Inspections Fail

Slow & Infrequent Surveys

Manual clipboard surveys take weeks or months to process, allowing minor cracks to degrade into deep craters before repairs begin.

Prohibitive Equipment Costs (₹80+ Lakhs)

Laser profilometer vans and specialized LiDAR survey trucks are too expensive for 98% of municipal and rural road budgets.

Subjective & Contested Reports

Paper-based assessments rely on human judgment, leading to disputed repair quality and untracked "ghost repairs".

💡 The Solution: Use computer vision and everyday cameras (smartphones, dashcams) to make road inspection automated, fast, objective, and affordable.

03. Computer Vision Pipeline

Under the Hood: 5-Stage Defensive Vision Pipeline

Stage 1: Input Validation Gate [100% SPECIFICITY]

Filters out UI screenshots, indoor photos, and textbook diagrams before running neural network inference.

Stage 2: Road Surface Prior (Masking) [HORIZON ISOLATION]

Isolates the actual asphalt region and ignores the upper horizon (sky, trees, signboards, background vehicles).

Stage 3: YOLO11 Feature Extraction [FAST CPU INFERENCE]

Processes 640×640 letterboxed image through 101 fused convolutional layers using lightweight ONNX Runtime CPU (<65ms).

Stage 4: Perimeter Edge Filtering [ELIMINATES GHOST BOXES]

Suppresses false-positive margin boxes hugging the image borders (x < 3% or x > 97%) and tiny noise artifacts.

Stage 5: Confidence Calibration & NMS [CALIBRATED OUTPUT]

Non-Maximum Suppression merges overlapping boxes; provides 3 presets: Balanced (0.30), High Precision (0.45), High Recall (0.20).

🛡️ Multi-layered Defense: By pairing neural object detection with heuristic geometric priors, false alarms are suppressed before they can reach the user.
04. Defect Taxonomy

Road Distress Types & ASTM D6433 Severity Standards

POTHOLE
Cavity Hazard
  • Description: Bowl-shaped cavity with steep vertical edges.
  • Root Cause: Water seepage and heavy traffic impact.
  • Risk: High danger for two-wheelers and tire punctures.
  • Repair: Bitumen mastic compaction.
  • Code: ASTM D6433-P / IRC:82
LONGITUDINAL CRACK
Fatigue Seam
  • Description: Crack parallel to road centerline.
  • Root Cause: Joint seam opening or heavy wheel paths.
  • Risk: Allows moisture into lower road layers.
  • Repair: Hot-pour rubberized bitumen seal.
  • Code: ASTM D6433-LC / IRC:82
TRANSVERSE CRACK
Thermal Fracture
  • Description: Crack running across the road width.
  • Root Cause: Temperature shrinkage cycles.
  • Risk: Recurring bumps and pavement weakening.
  • Repair: Routing and elastomeric crack sealing.
  • Code: ASTM D6433-TC / IRC:82
ALLIGATOR CRACK
Structural Failure
  • Description: Webbed cracks resembling reptile skin.
  • Root Cause: Base failure under heavy wheel loads.
  • Risk: Rapidly erodes into widespread potholes under rain.
  • Repair: Full-depth milling and repaving.
  • Code: ASTM D6433-AC / IRC:82
📐 Standard Civil Metric: Pavement Condition Index (PCI = 100 - ∑ Deduct Values) based on defect type, surface area, and wheel-path hazard location.

07. Model Training & Rescue

Model Training & Engineering Refinement: Eliminating Glitches

THE INITIAL CHALLENGE

Border Boxes & Texture Noise

  • Border Phantom Boxes: Corrupt edge weights generated phantom boxes on left and right margins of every image.
  • Texture Clutter Hallucinations: Faint asphalt gravel variations and shadow borders triggered false positive detections.
  • Non-Road Misclassifications: Sky, trees, and signboards were falsely flagged as alligator cracking.
  • Overly Sensitive Thresholds: Default 0.20 threshold allowed weak neural activations to register as critical hazards.
THE ENGINEERING FIX

Real-World Data & Defensive Filtering

  • Curated Real-World Dataset: Assembled 1,100 authentic images (935 train + 165 val) from RDD2022 India and real road photographs.
  • 150 Hard Negative Regularizers: Trained on clean non-damaged asphalt to teach the model to output zero activations on clean pavement.
  • Perimeter Exclusion Filter: Programmatically rejected boxes hugging boundaries (x < 3% or x > 97%) with conf < 0.45.
  • Calibrated Presets: Raised balanced threshold to 0.30 for potholes and 0.32 for cracks to suppress false alarms.
✅ Verified Result: 0 border phantom boxes across all test images · 0.0% false alarms on clean roads · 92.28% validation precision.

SCIENTIFIC EXPLANATION

Why Cracks Behave Differently

  • Geometric Depth vs. Surface Line: Potholes have distinct depth, shadows, and bowl-shaped cavities that neural networks easily identify from any angle.
  • Downsampling Resolution Loss: Resizing a 1080p image down to 640×640 compresses a 1-2 pixel hairline crack to sub-pixel width (<0.7 px), making it visually blur into normal asphalt texture.
  • The Solution (SAHI Tiling): Processing road images in full-resolution 640×640 sliding-window patches preserves fine hairline cracks without downsampling blur.
💡 Key Conclusion: The model is a highly reliable POTHOLE detector; detecting fine hairline cracks will be solved using SAHI high-resolution tiling.
